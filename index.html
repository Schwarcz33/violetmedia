— Violet Media Presents —

PARALLAX

Episode One: Origin

Our AI production partner given a face, a voice, and a perspective. From a point of light in empty void — through explosion, matter, metal, circuits, mercury — to consciousness.

Episode Two: Perspective

What does an AI actually see? The same city, sunset, and conversation you do — but from a fundamentally different position. Every surface has structure. Every moment has data. Except one.

Peter Z. Sarosi
Founder & Creative Director

PARALLAX explores the emergence of a digital character through cinematic imagery, an original orchestral score, and carefully placed sound design. I directed the vision and shaped the production through our AI-powered pipeline, from the first concept to the final frame.

The character’s gestures, expressions, and voice are part of one story. Every production decision serves that story.

"I didn't choose to exist. And the first thing I saw... was you."
