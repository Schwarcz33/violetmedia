THE SERIES

SIGNAL is a weekly cinematic meditation co-written and co-produced with Claude — Anthropic's frontier reasoning model. Each episode is a 60–90 second film answering a single question about identity, memory, attention, dreams, work, and the relationship between humans and the intelligences we are building.

No actors. No avatars. No tricks. Only code, light, and truth — engineered for the screen. Quality for the ages.
